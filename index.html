Financial Fraud Section 14 & 16 PECA

Bank Officer Spoof Call & OTP Interception

Location: Lahore | Authority: NCCIA Cyber Crime Wing

Scammers spoofed an official bank helpline number and coerced an elderly citizen into sharing their transaction OTP under the threat of account freezing.

👇 Click to read full case study details...
1. Incident Description: The victim received a call displaying the bank's official 111-helpline number (using VoIP caller ID spoofing). The scammer claimed an unauthorized transfer was occurring and asked for the OTP to "block" the transaction, resulting in a Rs. 850,000 fraud.
2. Legal Provisions Applied: PECA 2016 Section 14 (Electronic Fraud - 2 to 10 years imprisonment) and Section 16 (Unauthorized use of identity information).
3. Evidence Collected: Call log duration, telecom operator CDR (Call Detail Records), bank transaction IDs, and destination wallet CNIC details.
4. Outcome & Recovery: NCCIA issued a freeze order under Section 23 to State Bank of Pakistan. Intercepted destination accounts were frozen, Rs. 620,000 recovered, and 2 suspects arrested in a raid.
Harassment / Extortion Section 20 PECA

Social Media Photo Manipulation & Blackmail

Location: Karachi | Authority: NCCIA Forensics Unit

Perpetrator modified public social media pictures using basic AI editing tools and demanded money under threat of public release.

👇 Click to read full case study details...
1. Incident Description: A fake profile downloaded public photos of the victim, digitally edited them, and sent extortion messages via WhatsApp demanding payment via mobile wallet.
2. Legal Provisions Applied: PECA 2016 Section 20 (Offences against dignity of a natural person - up to 5 years imprisonment/fine) & Constitution Article 14 (Inviolability of Dignity).
3. Evidence Collected: Unedited original photos, WhatsApp chat export with timestamps, IP login logs obtained via platform metadata requests.
4. Outcome & Recovery: Digital forensics matched IP headers to a registered home broadband line. Suspect was arrested, digital devices seized, and fake accounts taken down via PTA portal.
