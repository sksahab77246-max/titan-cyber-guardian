Section 14 PECA Financial Scam

Bank Officer Spoof Call & OTP Theft

Location: Lahore | Authority: NCCIA Cyber Crime

Incident Details: Suspect spoofed official bank helpline numbers, convincing the victim that their account was blocked. Victim shared OTP.

Legal Action: Charged under PECA Section 14 (Electronic Fraud) and Section 16 (Unauthorized use of identity).

Outcome: NCCIA traced bank accounts, froze stolen funds via SBP coordination, and arrested the suspect.
Section 20 PECA Cyber Harassment

Social Media Photo Editing & Extortion

Location: Karachi | Authority: NCCIA Digital Forensics

Incident Details: Perpetrator edited public profile pictures of victim and threatened to leak them unless paid via mobile wallet.

Legal Action: PECA Section 20 (Cyber Harassment) and Article 14 of Constitution (Inviolability of Dignity).

Outcome: Digital forensics traced IP address and registered SIM. Perpetrator sentenced to 3 years imprisonment.
Section 16 PECA SIM Swap

Biometric Duplicate SIM Takeover

Location: Faisalabad | Authority: PTA & NCCIA

Incident Details: Franchise owner created duplicate SIM using unauthorized thumbprint scan to access WhatsApp.

Legal Action: PECA Section 16 & Section 17 (Unauthorized SIM issuance).

Outcome: Franchise license cancelled by PTA, offenders booked under non-bailable cybercrime sections.
