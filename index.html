    Reliable browser-based voice-clone detection needs specialized models TITAN doesn't carry. Use these red flags and real tools instead.

    🔊 Flat or robotic tone

    Cloned voices lack natural breathing pauses, micro-hesitations, or emotional variation.

    🎚️ Pitch/pacing glitches

    Sudden unnatural jumps in pitch or speed, especially at word boundaries.

    🌐 Background mismatch

    Cloned audio often has unnaturally clean or inconsistent background noise.

    ⏱️ Scripted urgency

    Voice scams rely on panic to stop you noticing audio flaws — slow down before reacting.

    Verified tools: Resemble Detect, Pindrop

    Real-time deepfake video detection needs frame-by-frame ML models. Use this checklist and escalate to NCCIA forensics for anything used as evidence.

    👁️ Unnatural blinking

    Metronomic blinking that looks too regular or too rare.

    💋 Lip-sync drift

    Watch mouth shapes against audio on consonants like "B," "M," "P."

    🔆 Lighting mismatch

    Soft "halo" edge around face/hairline, or shadows that don't match the scene.

    🧍 Rigid neck/body

    Face moves convincingly but neck, shoulders, or ears stay unnaturally still.

    Verified tools: Deepware Scanner, Hive Moderation, Content Credentials Verify

    Your Rights

    • Filing a complaint is free — no fee is charged.
    • You cannot be refused the right to file an FIR/complaint.
    • Real officers never send an "arrest warrant" over WhatsApp/call or demand money on a video call.
    • You can ask for your complaint's reference number and follow up on its status.
    • Providing evidence isn't mandatory but speeds up the investigation.
