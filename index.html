    AI-Generated Media Check

    Please read before using: Genuine, forensic-grade deepfake detection is a hard problem. TITAN provides an educational checklist and points to real verification tools. For legal evidence, request verification through NCCIA/FIA's digital forensics lab.

    ✋ Hands, ears, teeth

    AI images often go wrong here — extra/fused fingers, odd ears, uniform teeth.

    🔆 Lighting & shadows

    Check if shadows fall consistently across the image and eye reflections match.

    🧵 Background & text

    Background patterns and text/signage are often garbled in AI images.

    🪞 Skin & edges

    Unnaturally smooth skin or blurred edges where a subject meets the background.

    🔊 Flat tone

    Cloned voices lack natural breathing pauses or emotional variation.

    🎚️ Pitch glitches

    Listen for sudden jumps in pitch or speaking speed.

    🌐 Background mismatch

    Cloned audio often has an unnaturally clean or inconsistent background.

    ⏱️ Scripted urgency

    Scammers rely on panic to stop you from noticing audio flaws.

    👁️ Unnatural blinking

    Deepfakes under/over-blink or blink metronomically.

    💋 Lip-sync drift

    Watch mouth shapes against audio, sync often drifts mid-clip.

    🔆 Lighting mismatch

    Face lighting that doesn't match the rest of the scene.

    🧍 Rigid motion

    Face moves but neck, shoulders, or ears stay unnaturally still.
